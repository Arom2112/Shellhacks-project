Method, sources & caveats

Crash severity weights: fatal 25, serious injury 12, other injury 5 or 2, property-damage-only 1. Open pothole reports add 3; closed or other reports add 1. Each route counts a grid cell once.

This is a statistical demo, not real-time navigation. Crash history ends in 2018. Potholes use Miami-Dade County’s 2023 snapshot and emphasize county-maintained roads, excluding most municipal streets.

Sources: FDOT Crashes_All · Miami-Dade 311. Routes are requested from the public OSRM demo server only after you press the route button.
