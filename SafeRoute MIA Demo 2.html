Method, sources & caveats

Predicted risk model: per-cell, severity-weighted crashes by year with exponential recency weighting (2.5-year half-life), a least-squares trend factor constrained to 0.5–1.5, and confirmed-open potholes. Each route counts a grid cell once.

Holdout test: trained on 2011–2015 and compared with actual 2016–2018 crashes. Rank correlation was 0.923 and top-decile overlap was 88%.

Risk is forecast from historical trend, not a live incident feed. Crashes come from FDOT 2011–2018. Potholes use Miami-Dade County’s 311 2023 snapshot; only LOCKED reports (confirmed not yet fixed) are included. CLOSED, DUP, and CANCEL reports are excluded. County 311 data emphasizes county-maintained roads and excludes most municipal streets.

Sources: FDOT Crashes_All · Miami-Dade 311. Routes are requested only after you press the route button: Google Routes when you provide a key, with the public OSRM demo server as fallback.
