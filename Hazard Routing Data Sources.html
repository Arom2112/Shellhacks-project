What is actually usable

Filter by role. “Verified live” means the research session fetched the service or official page successfully on Sep 26, 2026.

01

FDOT Crashes_All

● verified live

The statistical-hotspot backbone: roughly 3.3 million statewide crash points across 2011–2019, with 2019 partial and 2018 the latest full year.

BEST FITcrash risk
Useful fields

FDOT and officer coordinates, crash date/time, county, roadway and intersection names, route identifiers, functional class.

Access

Public ArcGIS REST. Query and export CSV, GeoJSON, shapefile. No key. Paginate past the 1,000-record limit.

Caveat

Excellent for slow-changing hotspots, not current incidents. Dedicated layers cover fatalities, injuries, pedestrians, speeding, work zones, and more.

Open FeatureServer ↗ Miami-Dade filter: COUNTY_TXT='MIAMI-DADE'
02

Miami-Dade 311 · 2023

● verified live

The best public pothole and road-defect proxy: ticket-level requests with latitude, longitude, dates, priority, and open/closed status.

BEST FITpotholes
Useful fields

issue_type, issue_description, ticket status, created/updated/closed timestamps, street address, city, priority, latitude and longitude.

Access

Public ArcGIS REST. Query and extract CSV, GeoJSON, shapefile or KML. No key. Paginate beyond 1,000 rows.

Caveat

Latest snapshot is 2023, county-maintained work emphasis, approximate locations, BETA. Probe exact pothole issue codes before the bulk pull.

Open FeatureServer ↗ FIU Modesto Maidique is in the covered unincorporated area

Google Maps Platform

● pricing verified

Routes supplies candidate polylines; Roads can supply speed limits; Places or Geocoding handles destinations. The hazard penalty still belongs to your app.

ROUTINGgeometry + map

index-corroborated

Suggested architecture

Precompute hazards. Score routes fast.

Do not call FDOT or county 311 on every route request. Pull once, normalize once, and serve a small static risk layer.

STEP 1

Ingest

Paginate Miami-Dade crashes and pothole-like 311 records. Retain coordinates, dates, severity/status, and source IDs.

→
STEP 2

Aggregate

Bin points into a geographic grid, weight serious crashes more heavily, and save compact GeoJSON or JSON.

→
STEP 3

Compare

Request alternate routes, sample each polyline against hazard cells, and rank safety against added travel time.

routeRisk = Σ(
  crashDensity × severityWeight
  + potholeDensity × defectWeight
)

recommendation = lowestRisk(
  candidateRoutes
)

Keep the claim honest

Call it a historical hazard score, not a guarantee of safety. Crash history ends at the latest full FDOT year, 2018; county road complaints end at the 2023 snapshot.

Weekend plan

Sequence the risk out of the build

Data first, route display second, polish last. The biggest avoidable failure is spending Saturday on map styling before the pipeline works.

Saturday CORE PIPELINE

  1. 1
    Set up routing early

    Create the Google billing/key setup first, or commit to OSRM/Valhalla immediately.

  2. 2
    Pull and shrink FDOT data

    Fetch Miami-Dade rows, then aggregate to hazard cells. The research estimate is about two hours.

  3. 3
    Probe the 311 taxonomy

    Run a distinct-values query, confirm pothole-like issue types, and combine with issue-description text search.

  4. 4
    Draw alternate routes

    Get at least two candidates between the same origin and destination before tuning any score.

Sunday DEMO + POLISH

  1. 5
    Score the candidates

    Sample route geometry against the grid and expose both hazard score and added travel time.

  2. 6
    Make the tradeoff visible

    Show “fastest” and “lower historical risk” side by side. Explain why the recommendation changed.

  3. 7
    Add one credibility layer

    Optional: FARS fatal-crash overlay or Vision Zero comparison, only after the main flow is stable.

  4. 8
    State the limits in the demo

    Historical crashes, 2023 road complaints, county coverage emphasis, and no guarantee of present road conditions.

Gaps & open questions

Know what not to promise

These are genuine data gaps from the research, not implementation bugs.

Exact pothole issue codes

The 311 schema is verified, but the exact issue_type values still need one distinct-values query.

PROBE FIRST
Live City of Miami 311 URL

The city inventory says the daily public layer exists; the endpoint was not verified.

OPTIONAL LOOKUP
School-zone geometry

No public Miami-Dade polygon or point dataset was found. Nearby school places are only a proxy.

LABEL AS PROXY
Bulk speed limits

No open per-segment FDOT download was found. Google Roads or OpenStreetMap maxspeed tags are the practical options.

VERIFY ACCESS
Current road conditions

The strongest county dataset is a 2023 snapshot. Do not market the route as real-time pothole avoidance.

USE “HISTORICAL”
Best pitch language:

“We rank alternate routes using public crash history and reported road-defect density, then explain the safety/time tradeoff.”
